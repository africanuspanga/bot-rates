## LinkedIn

**We've made the Bank of Tanzania's daily exchange rates free for every developer and business in Tanzania.**

Every trading day, BOT publishes reference rates for 44 currencies on its website. There's no official API, so banks, fintechs, accountants, bureaux de change and online shops copy those numbers by hand.

Exchange Rates fixes that:
• A free JSON and CSV API: no key, no sign-up
• Every BOT rate since June 1999, one file per year or per currency
• A dollar ⇄ shilling converter at the BOT rate
• A website widget bureaus can paste in one line, with their own margin
• llms.txt and an OpenAPI spec, so AI assistants can quote the right rate with its date

Who it's for: VAT and customs conversions, fintech and mobile money, bureau websites, payroll and school fees set in USD, researchers and journalists.

Try it: https://www.driftmark.co.tz/exchange-rates

If you build something with it, tell us. We'd love to feature it.

*Exchange Rates is an independent project by Driftmark Technologies. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Bank of Tanzania. Rates are BOT reference rates for information only; banks and bureaux set their own prices.*
