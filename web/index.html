    Who it’s for

    One rate, many jobs

    BOT publishes one official reference rate per day. That is exactly what these people need, and most of them still copy it by hand.

    Tax and customs

    Convert invoices, VAT and import values at the BOT rate for the right day, inside accounting and clearing software.

    Fintech and mobile money

    Show the official rate next to your own, benchmark FX spreads and pull history for compliance reports.

    Bureaux de change

    Paste one line and the shop’s website shows today’s buy and sell prices with your own margin.

    Shops, hotels and tours

    Quote in dollars, show the shilling price automatically. Safari operators and online stores stay current.

    Payroll, fees and rent

    Salaries, school fees and leases set in USD get converted at a rate everyone can verify.

    Research and journalism

    Chart the shilling since 1999 for stories, theses and policy work. CSV for every year.

    Spreadsheets

    Load today’s rates or a whole year into Google Sheets or Excel with a single formula.

    AI agents and chat bots

    A plain-text guide and OpenAPI spec let ChatGPT, Claude and WhatsApp bots answer “dola leo?” correctly.

    Developers

    The API

    Plain JSON and CSV over HTTPS. Open endpoints work today with no key. Personal API keys with usage stats are coming with exchangerates.co.tz.

      Built for AI agents

      Point any assistant at llms.txt or load openapi.json as a tool. Every response says its source, date and units, so agents don’t guess.

      No code

      Website widget

      Choose currencies and your margin, then paste one line into any website. It refreshes by itself every day.
