    Who uses one daily rate

    BOT publishes one reference rate per currency per day. Plenty of work in Tanzania depends on it, and most of it still copies the number by hand.

    • Tax and customs

      Convert invoices, VAT and import values at the BOT rate for the right date

    • Fintech and mobile money

      Show the reference rate next to your own and keep history for compliance reports

    • Bureaux de change

      Show buy and sell prices on the shop’s website with your own margin on top

    • Shops, hotels and tours

      Quote in dollars and show the shilling price beside it

    • Payroll, fees and rent

      Convert USD salaries, school fees and leases at a rate both sides can check

    • Research and journalism

      27 years of daily rates as CSV, one file per year

    • Spreadsheets

      Load the latest rates into Google Sheets or Excel with one formula

    • AI assistants and chat bots

      A plain-text guide and an OpenAPI spec so a bot can answer “dola leo?” with a dated BOT rate

    API

    Plain JSON and CSV over HTTPS. No key and no sign-up for these endpoints. Personal API keys with usage stats are planned.

      For AI assistants

      Point an assistant at llms.txt or load openapi.json as a tool. Every response carries its BOT date and units.

      Website widget

      Choose currencies and your margin, then paste one line into any website. It reads the latest BOT rates each time the page loads.
