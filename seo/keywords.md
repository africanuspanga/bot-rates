# Keyword research: Exchange Rates (Tanzania)

Collected 5 Nov 2026. Two sources:
- **Google Ads Keyword Planner** (CV CHAP CHAP account, location Tanzania, all languages, Google Search, Oct 2025 – Sep 2026): average monthly searches, competition and top-of-page bid ranges.
- **Google autocomplete** (gl=tz, English): what people type after each starting phrase, in Google's order.

Google Ads only shows volume *ranges* (e.g. 10K – 100K) for accounts without active spend.

### What it says

- **The converter is the big opportunity.** "dollar to tsh" (100K – 1M) plus "usd to tzs" and "tzs to usd" (10K – 100K each) are far bigger than anything about BOT or APIs.
- **BOT-branded searches are steady** ("bot exchange rate", "bot exchange rate today": 1K – 10K each), and people want the *previous* rates ("bot exchange rate previous": 100 – 1K).
- **Swahili** is real but smaller: "bei ya dola leo" 100 – 1K.
- **Developers** searching "exchange rate api" are few (10 – 100), so the API grows through word of mouth, LinkedIn and X, not search.
- **Competition is Low** across the board and bids are cheap ($0.04 – $1.80), so a small Google Ads test on "dollar to tsh" would be inexpensive if you ever want one.
- Not targeted on purpose: "crdb exchange rate", "nmb exchange rate" (other banks' brands; our pages don't have their rates).
